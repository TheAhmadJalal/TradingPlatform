Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about the platform, fees, security and withdrawals.

1 What is Cedar Capital Group?

Cedar Capital Group is an investment platform that uses advanced algorithms to analyse the financial markets and identify growth opportunities.

2 How can I start investing?
  1. Create an account on our platform.
  2. Complete your investor profile.
  3. Make a first deposit through our secure interface.
  4. Our algorithm starts working for you in real time.
3 What are the fees?

Our fee structure is transparent and competitive:

  • Management fee: 1.5% per year on invested capital.
  • Performance fee: 10% on realised gains.
  • Withdrawal fee: 4% of the amount withdrawn.
4 Are my funds safe?

Absolutely. We use institutional-grade security measures:

  • 95% of funds held in cold (offline) wallets.
  • Two-factor authentication (2FA) for account access.
  • Regular security audits by independent firms.
  • Compliance with international anti-money-laundering regulations.
5 Can I withdraw my funds at any time?

Yes, you can request a withdrawal at any time from your client interface.

6 What is the trading algorithm?

Our algorithm is an artificial intelligence system that analyses thousands of financial data points in real time.

7 Do I need financial knowledge to invest?

No. Our platform is designed to be intuitive and accessible to beginners and experienced investors alike.

8 What are the historical returns?

Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results.
